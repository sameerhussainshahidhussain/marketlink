Part 1: Administrator Operations & Roles

Route Prefix: /admin/* • Controlled by role:admin middleware

The administrator functions as the market governance body. MarketLink does not allow arbitrary sellers to post unverified goods. The administrator protects consumers, regulates food standards, and manages physical market coordination.

1. Farmer Vetting & Approval

When a new grower signs up at /register/farmer, their profile defaults to status = 'pending'.

  • Admin accesses Admin → Farmers → Pending list.
  • Admin reviews farm business name, contact info, and production background.
  • Clicking Approve sets the farmer to active. Only then can they create products and pickup slots.
  • Admin can also Suspend or reactivate farmers anytime if quality issues arise.
2. Market Venues & Coordinates

Admin manages all weekend market venues displayed on the interactive Leaflet map.

  • Create new markets with City, Address, Operating Days (e.g., Saturday & Sunday), and Timings.
  • Input precise Latitude & Longitude so customers get exact map pins.
  • Assign attending farmers to specific venues so shoppers know which stalls to visit.
  • Toggle market status between active and inactive.
3. Customer Review Moderation

To prevent fake reviews, all feedback submitted after order completion passes through admin moderation.

  • Admin views pending reviews submitted by verified buyers.
  • Admin can Approve constructive reviews to publish them on product/farmer pages.
  • Admin can Reject or delete spam/inappropriate submissions.
4. Sales Trends & CSV Export

Admin maintains complete financial and volume oversight without handling cash directly.

  • View platform-wide revenue graphs and daily order trends.
  • Track best-selling produce categories and top-performing farm stalls.
  • Filter by date range and generate downloadable CSV audit reports.

Part 2: Vendor (Farmer) Operations & Roles

Route Prefix: /farmer/* • Controlled by role:farmer & farmer.approved

The farmer portal gives local growers direct control over their harvest availability. Farmers eliminate agricultural waste by knowing their exact order volume before driving to the weekend venue.

Live Order Lifecycle Workflow:
Step 1
Placed

Customer reserves items before cutoff deadline. Stock is locked.

Step 2
Accepted

Farmer accepts the order. Customer receives live notification.

Step 3
Ready for Pickup

Crate is packed and waiting at the market stall counter.

Step 4
Completed

Shopper arrives, inspects produce, and pays cash on site.

Product & Stock Management

Vendors can add, edit, or toggle items with custom measurement units (kg, bunch, jar, punnet, box):

  • Automatic availability badges (In Stock > 5 units, Low Stock 1-5 units, Sold Out 0 units).
  • Ability to upload clean harvest images and set pricing per unit.
  • One-click stock toggle to temporarily hide out-of-season produce.
Pickup Slots & Cutoff Timers

Vendors decide exactly when and where customers can collect their crates:

  • Create slots for upcoming market days (e.g. Saturday 8 AM – 12 PM).
  • Set Cutoff Timers (e.g. Friday 8 PM). After cutoff, shoppers cannot order or cancel, giving the farmer time to harvest.
  • Set capacity limits (e.g. max 25 orders per pickup window).
Weekly Stock Templates

Farmers don't need to re-enter their crops every single week:

  • Save recurring harvest configurations as templates (e.g. "Standard Saturday Morning Harvest").
  • Load template with 1 click to restore inventory for the upcoming weekend.
Customer Review Replies

Vendors build long-term customer relationships:

  • View verified reviews submitted for orders at their stall.
  • Post public vendor replies thanking customers or giving storage tips.
